Championships: C Wrecking · N Stockcar · Enter next race after results · R restart the unfinished race · Esc leave. Tracks follow the original schedule; completed points are retained through restarts.
Races: F5 Wrecking Racing/Arena · F6 Stockcar (circuits) · F8 Time Trial (one car, unlimited laps) · F9 Total Destruction (arenas) · F7 leave and show results. The countdown locks throttle until GO. You are highlighted in yellow in the results; R restarts.
Free driving: Enter start/leave · W/↑ accelerate · S/↓ reverse · A/D or arrow keys steer · Space brake · P pause · R return to start.
Damage: the front faces up; green/yellow/red shows increasing damage, and the bar shows engine health. PTS: accident points · KO: destroyed opponents. Reset also clears damage and points.
Music: open a local Redbook track · F10 pause/resume music. Game pause and focus loss also pause music.
Track/model view: ← → rotate · ↑ ↓ tilt · WASD pan · mouse wheel or +/− zoom · R reset · Tab switch view · Page Up/Page Down switch track
